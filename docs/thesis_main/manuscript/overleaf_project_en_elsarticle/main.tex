\title{Auditable Adaptive Panoramic Layout Annotation: Calibration-Supported Worker Profiling and Conditional Routing}

\author[inst1]{Author names withheld for drafting}
\address[inst1]{Affiliation withheld for drafting}

\begin{abstract}
Panoramic room-layout annotation combines geometric judgement, interface operation, and correction of model-generated proposals. A worker who performs well overall may nevertheless fail predictably on a particular risk family, while a detailed conditional profile may be too weakly supported to guide deployment. We study when calibrated evidence about worker performance should influence task routing. The proposed framework separates three components---reference-based accuracy, stable peer compatibility, and worker-attributable structural invalidity---and estimates them from an independent calibration stage with partial pooling and explicit support diagnostics. It then constructs a calibrated global ranking and enables conditional routing only for routable failure modes that cross a predeclared calibration-support boundary; unsupported cases fall back to the global policy. A supporting T1 experiment evaluates model-assistance heterogeneity, whereas a prospective V1 experiment compares conditional and global routing under symmetric operations, two safety non-inferiority gates, and a delivery-adjusted quality hierarchy. \expectedresult{} The marked result scaffold anticipates non-redundant and cross-stage-stable profile components, supported conditional effects, preserved quality with reduced active time in T1, and safety followed by quality and cost gains in V1; all corresponding sample sizes, effects, and intervals remain \tbddata{}. The contribution is therefore an auditable evidence contract linking worker characterization, calibration support, fallback behaviour, and prospective policy evaluation rather than an unrestricted claim that finer personalization is always beneficial.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item Separates three complementary components of panoramic-layout worker performance.
\item Restricts conditional routing to effects supported by independent calibration evidence.
\item Evaluates routing prospectively with symmetric operations and explicit safety gates.
\end{highlights}
