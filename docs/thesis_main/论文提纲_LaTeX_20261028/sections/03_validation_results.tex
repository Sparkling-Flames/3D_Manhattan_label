\paragraph{Geometric quality measurement}
Controlled tests confirm that $Q$ detects local height distortion even when mean height is unchanged, while remaining invariant under collinear resampling of unchanged wall geometry (Figure~\ref{fig:height-control}). In evaluations of 60 real annotations, 8 randomly invited annotators reported visible defects associated with the corresponding geometric measurements and structural diagnostics (Table~\ref{tab:quality-feedback}). Together, the controlled tests and defect feedback validate the geometric interpretation of $Q$: its measurements respond to the visible errors they are designed to capture. Supplement A presents the controlled tests, C16 details defect feedback and score-scale sensitivity, and D5 reports complementary overall-grade comparisons.

\paragraph{Paired-corner structural consensus}
\label{sec:method-validation}
In the shared-team example in Supplement C16, both BEV region voting \cite{lee2018} and paired-corner consensus retain the supported recess. Paired-corner consensus additionally records its supporters and aggregates their upper--lower endpoints to represent local wall heights. The fixed-size bathroom example shows how membership alone alters support for the same recess and determines whether it is retained (Figure~\ref{fig:bathroom-turn-votes}). Supplement C16 reports the shared-team comparison and support counts; Supplement D provides further upper-boundary evidence.
