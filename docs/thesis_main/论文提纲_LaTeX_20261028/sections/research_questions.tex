\subsection{Research questions}
\label{sec:research-questions}
The framework supports four questions about how human observations form collective layouts. Manual submissions, drawn independently from scratch with an interactive 3D preview, support controlled team recombination. Semi submissions record responses to initialized geometry.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{RQ1: Individual expression.} How do structural choices, boundary positions, quality and effort vary across annotators and visual conditions, and how do annotators retain, edit or correct initialization errors?
\item \textbf{RQ2: Group size.} How does team expansion affect mean quality, lower-tail quality, between-team variation and complete, evaluable output?
\item \textbf{RQ3: Group composition.} How do class proportions, specific members and retained teammates shape collective structure and quality?
\item \textbf{RQ4: Cross-view repetition and transfer.} Which individual habits and team outcomes recur across views of the same room, and which historical signals transfer?
\end{itemize}
