# Bounded corner-distance personnel experiments

## Finding

A stable universal partition is not supported. Original Rnew2 is independently reproduced and is a useful conditional two-class reference. The new legacy/mixed single-person corner-risk candidates provide modest history/discovery separation, but their fine memberships are less stable. The frozen discovery-nominated legacy meanK3 fails to improve the reused-assessment m2 shared-library outcomes. This negative transfer remains part of the record; the direct m2 extension is separately registered and trained only on history.

## Mixed-history comparison with original Rnew2

All six mixed m1 candidates have smaller matched held-out between-minus-within gaps than fold-refitted Rnew2 (about −.00046 to−.00100). K4 modestly lowers within distance, but does not jointly improve separation; this is a trade-off rather than a superior fine hierarchy.

## Block probes and support

The frozen reused-assessment probe contains 2,552 own-rule ABCs over 200 nested paths and a separate category-independent library with 152 anchors/63,476 disjoint pairs. Own-rule paths retain fixed A/B/C exchanged blocks and nested common core for every feasible integer n5–17. Cross-method comparisons use the shared endpoint library, not differently sampled own-rule curves. Exact metrics and support are in audit_corner/fresh_blocks outputs.

The new direct m2 training panel is label-independent and history-only: 20 images, ten buildings,160anchors,n5/9/13/17,two core paths,7,670native teams,144,140 disjoint pairs. It is the sole registered block-level extension, using two K models and bounded fixed-start optimization. Registration and native request hashes precede fitting. All structural failures and missing distances remain explicit.
